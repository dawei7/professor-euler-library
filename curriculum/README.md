# Professor Euler Curriculum

Workbooks from Grade 6 to MSc Mathematics, written for Professor Euler. `catalog/curriculum.json` lists every course,
its prerequisites, units and topics, and the workbooks published so far; the app downloads them from the release
"curriculum".

Unlike the books of the library, the workbooks are **not** open-licensed: Copyright © David Schmid, all rights
reserved. They are made available for personal, non-commercial use in the Professor Euler app only; copying,
redistribution and text and data mining are not permitted (each workbook carries its own notice).
