# Your mathematics learning paths

These five paths share one library. A book completed on one path does not need to be repeated on another. The order builds prerequisites and changes emphasis as you progress; it is a recommended study sequence, not a claim that every adjacent book is a strict prerequisite or that the path awards a degree.

For the English university paths, complete S.01–S.04 first or bring equivalent algebra, geometry, trigonometry and precalculus knowledge. The Complete Mathematics path already includes these books.

Solve exercises actively, compare with source-provided answers only after your own attempt, and revisit gaps. Available answers are not necessarily complete worked solutions to every exercise.

## Choose a book

Every book has its own page in [docs/books](books/), with an essay that says what the book trains, why the subject is worth knowing, which other books in this library it honestly depends on, and how its chapters or lectures actually proceed. The walks follow the bundle outlines. A lecture course is described by the lecture titles the bundle records. Where the outline leaves a run of lectures untitled, or a slide set does not yield readable text, the essay says so instead of inventing a syllabus.

This page is the path guide: what each of the five paths trains, and why its books stand in that order. In Professor Euler, My books > Get books downloads a whole path or single books; the seven High school and first university books come with the app. A book completed on one path is the same book on another.
